What is ZCOMMERZ?

ZCOMMERZ lets you build and run an online store in Bangladesh: themes, products, payments, delivery and sales channels in one place.

Can I build my store from my Facebook page?

Yes. Connect your page, review what comes across, pick a theme and publish. You can edit everything afterwards.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Everything is point and click.

Can I sell on Daraz and WhatsApp too?

Yes. Connect Daraz and WhatsApp from the dashboard and manage them alongside your store.

How do customers pay?

Through SSLCOMMERZ: Visa, Mastercard, mobile wallets like bKash and Bangla QR, 36+ channels in total.
